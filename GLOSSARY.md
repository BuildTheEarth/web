# BuildTheEarth Web Platform

The web platform coordinates regional build teams, geospatial territory claims, builder applications, and community showcases for the 1:1 scale recreation of planet Earth in Minecraft.

## Language

### Core Organizations & Territory

**Build Team**:
An autonomous regional organization responsible for coordinating builders and managing claims within a specific real-world geographic territory.
_Avoid_: Group, project, sub-team, region guild

**Claim**:
A designated geographic polygon on the real-world Earth map assigned to a builder or build team for recreation in Minecraft.
_Avoid_: Plot, region, zone, land, parcel

### Content & Media Assets

**Showcase**:
A curated highlight of a completed or notable build within a claim or build team, featured publicly with imagery.
_Avoid_: Portfolio item, gallery post, spotlight

**Upload**:
A stored binary media file (such as an in-game screenshot) associated with a showcase or claim.
_Avoid_: Attachment, screenshot, asset, picture

**Calendar Event**:
A scheduled public or team-specific building session, contest, or milestone event.
_Avoid_: Schedule entry, meetup, session, contest

### People & Roles

**User**:
An authenticated account identified via single sign-on (SSO) with linked Discord and Minecraft credentials.
_Avoid_: Account, player, visitor

**Member**:
A user who belongs to a specific build team.
_Avoid_: Team affiliate, participant

**Builder**:
A user actively assigned to work on one or more claims or authorized by a build team to construct within its territories.
_Avoid_: Worker, architect, craftsman

**Reviewer**:
A build team member authorized to inspect and accept or decline incoming builder applications.
_Avoid_: Evaluator, judge, moderator

### Applications & Onboarding

**Application**:
A structured request submitted by a user to join a build team, consisting of responses to team-configured questions.
_Avoid_: Submission, audition, enrollment form, ticket

**Trial**:
A probationary evaluation state granted to an applicant who must demonstrate building competence before receiving full membership.
_Avoid_: Audition, probation, test run

**Response Template**:
A reusable preset message used by reviewers when communicating application review outcomes to applicants.
_Avoid_: Canned response, boilerplate, snippet
